Direction B · Chosen

Electric Beauty

#2B3BD6#FF6B2C#FFE27A#FBF6EC#16143D

Why it wins: nobody in Indian beauty owns blue (Nykaa is pink, Tira red, Purplle purple, Sephora black and white), so it’s instantly ownable. Cobalt is the complement of the illustrations’ warm skin and pink flowers, so they pop rather than blend. The indigo/kohl associations make it culturally rooted, not just trendy. And tangerine plus butter keep it joyful rather than corporate.
