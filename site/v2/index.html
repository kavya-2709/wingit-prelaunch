Why try first

Shade

02 · Skincare

Texture

Perfume changes on skin over hours. Wear two, one on each wrist, and keep the one you can’t stop smelling.

Our story

It started at home. For both of us.

We spoke with around 200 people with the same problem: nobody wanted to be stuck with something that didn’t suit them. So we built the trial first.

Before you ask

Straight answers.

Do I pay anything online?

Nothing upfront. We bring full products and single-use testers home. You try, then pay only for what you keep. No order minimums, and no penalty if you keep nothing.

Where do the products come from?

From authorised brand stores and leading beauty chains, with a valid invoice for every item. No dupes, no grey-market stock.

What exactly do I get to try?

For every product you order, we carry a tester of the same item alongside the sealed full-size. You try the tester with a fresh Q-tip or sponge, then decide whether to buy the sealed one. We don’t send sachets or minis.

Who are the beauty partners?

Trained female beauty advisors. They can help with application, suggest pairings or give you an in-depth take on a product, but only if you want them to. They help you decide; they never push.

How does scheduling work?

Once ordering opens, you place an order and we get in touch to schedule your visit.

Where will Wingit be available?

We’re going live in NCR first. Join the list and tell us where you are; it helps us plan where to begin.
