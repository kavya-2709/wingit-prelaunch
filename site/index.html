How a Wingit trial works

The beauty counter comes home. The pressure doesn’t.

The clean-tester rule

Would you let a stranger put it on you?

Neither would we.

Beauty is personal, and it should be hygienic. No shared applicators. No double-dipping. That’s something a busy beauty counter can’t promise.

    Every tester is opened with a fresh, sealed Q-tip or sponge.

  • Disposed right after

The swatch journal

Three things a screen can’t tell you.

Wingit starts with three categories — makeup, skincare and fragrance — because each one only tells the truth on skin.

i.

Shade

A lipstick that’s perfect on a model can read brick-red on you.

Undertones shift between a phone screen and real skin, and between morning light and the office. So we bring a few shades, and you put them next to each other on your own lips, cheeks and wrist.

ii.

Texture

How it sinks in matters as much as what’s in it.

Sensitive, reactive or pigmented skin can’t afford guesswork. Feel the finish and see how your skin takes it before a full jar comes home with you.

Scent

Fragrance is the hardest thing to buy blind.

A perfume changes once it meets your skin and settles over a few hours. Wear two on different wrists, live with them for an afternoon, and keep the one you can’t stop smelling.

Our story

It started at home. For both of us.

Nikunj’s mum

For years she’d fill a shopping cart with serums and foundations, sit on it for ten minutes, then close the laptop. She has hyperpigmentation, so products people swear by often leave dark patches or settle into the wrong undertone — and running her own business, she rarely has time to test things in a store.

Saksham

“I have eczema. There’s a long list of things I can’t put on my skin — the wrong one, and my face flares up for weeks. My skin tone also doesn’t match what most brands call universal.”

We spoke with around 200 people who had the same problem: nobody wanted to be stuck with something that didn’t suit them. So we built the trial first.

Before you ask

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I pay anything online?

Nothing upfront. We bring full products and single-use testers home — you try, then pay only for what you keep. No order minimums, and no penalty if you keep nothing.

Where do the products come from?

From authorised brand stores and leading beauty chains, with a valid invoice for every item. No dupes, no grey-market stock.

What exactly do I get to try?

For every product you order, we carry a tester of the same item alongside the sealed full-size. You try the tester with a fresh Q-tip or sponge, then decide whether to buy the sealed one. We don’t send sachets or minis.

Who are the beauty partners?

Trained female beauty advisors. They can help with application, suggest pairings or give you an in-depth take on a product — only if you want them to. They help you decide, but never push.

How does scheduling work?

Once ordering opens, you place an order and we get in touch to schedule your visit.
